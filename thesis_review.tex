\section{\protect\Evaluation} 
\begin{center}

% These tables work in a way that first value is a grade and second is the additional commentary

% How demanding was the assigned project? Grade is A-E.
\AssignmentTable{A}{
  The assignment is demanding. It combines signal analysis with reproducible research software development, and either topic alone would be sufficient for a Bachelor thesis in my opinion.
}
\vspace{0.5cm}

% How well does the thesis fulfil the assigned task? Have the primary goals been achieved? Which assigned tasks have been incompletely covered, and which parts of the thesis are overextended? Justify your answer. Grade is A-D.
\FullfilmentTable{C}{
  The main goal of the assignment, "to analyse experimental data about location and signal strength from the flight of autonomous helicopters with the focus on reproducible research" was fullfiled, except for the "reproducible research principles" part. The four sub-goals listed in the assignment also have been addressed, but only partially. Therefore, despite the significant amount of work done, I cannot say that the goals were fully achieved.
}
\vspace{0.5cm}
 
% REVIEWER:
% Comment on the correctness of the approach and/or the solution methods. Grade is A-D.
% SUPERVISOR:
% Assess whether the student had a positive approach, whether the time limits were met, whether the conception was regularly consulted and whether the student was well prepared for the consultations. Assess the student’s ability to work independently. Grade is A-F.
\ActivityMethodologyTable{B}{
  The signal-analysis methodology seems correct and is supported by the results. However, the software implementation choices are questionable. One thesis goal is to "Discuss existing tools for reproducible research support and, if necessary, propose and implement a new system." I would expect Section 2.5 to justify the need for a new system and explain the software choices, but it does not.
}

\vspace{0.5cm}

% Is the thesis technically sound? How well did the student employ expertise in his/her field of study? Does the student explain clearly what he/she has done? Grade is A-F.
\TechnicalTable{C}{
  The thesis is technically sound, and the student seems to understand ranging methods well. However, Section 2.5.2 uses confusing terminology and seems out of place, while Sections 2.5.3 to 2.5.5 are shallow and incomplete. Another issue is the machine learning evaluation: although the test data are held out during model training, they are used for hyperparameter ($k$) tuning, making the reported results more even questionable than the thesis admits. Finally, the limitations described in Section 6.2.1 are significant and not clearly justified.
}
\vspace{0.5cm}

% Are formalisms and notations used properly? Is the thesis organized in a logical way? Is the thesis sufficiently extensive? Is the thesis well-presented? Is the language clear and understandable? Is the English satisfactory? Grade is A-F
\FormalTable{B}{
  The mathematics and notation are mostly fine. The language is clear and understandable, with only a few minor typos. For future work, I suggest reducing the use of passive voice. The main weakness is organization: some paragraphs are misplaced, e.g., the second half of Section 2.2 is clearly not related work.
}
\vspace{0.5cm}

% Does the thesis make adequate reference to earlier work on the topic? Was the selection of sources adequate? Is the student’s original work clearly distinguished from earlier work in the field? Do the bibliographic citations meet the standards? Grade is A-F
\SourcesTable{C}{
  Overall, the number and quality of references seem good. What is missing is deeper integration of the references in the related work section. This section contains only a small number of references, and some claims are unsupported (e.g., Section 2.1).
}

\vspace{0.5cm}

% Comment on the overall quality of the thesis, its novelty and its impact on the field, its strengths and weaknesses, the utility of the solution that is presented, the theoretical/formal level, the student’s skillfulness, etc.

  I would expect a thesis focused on reproducible research to include a public repository for both code and data. Additionally, the GUI images in Section 6 are unreadable.
}

\section{\protect\Overall} 
% Summarize your opinion on the thesis and explain your final grading.
% \newline

Considering the overall challenging goals, I grade the thesis \textbf{\generalgrade{B}}.

\vspace{0.5cm}

Question 1: Even after the improvements achieved in this thesis, the absolute error still seems too high, in meters, which is a limitation acknowledged in the conclusion. 
Do you see further improvement options with the potential to reduce the error by at least an order of magnitude? 
Or is this a fundamental limitation of RSSI, and we should consider a different approach?

\vspace{0.5cm}

Question 2: One thesis goal was to follow reproducible research principles.
However, the provided code targets a very narrow audience: Windows is the only supported platform, \texttt{uv} is required, and the implementation depends on a VSCode plugin. Since the rationale behind these choices is not described in the thesis, what strategy did you use to choose technologies for your implementation?
